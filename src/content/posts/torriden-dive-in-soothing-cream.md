I have been using the Torriden DIVE-IN Low Molecular Hyaluronic Acid Soothing Cream for about three months. I am currently on my second tube, although this is not technically a repurchase—I originally bought a two-pack.

My skin is dry and somewhat sensitive, but I do not always enjoy thick, heavy moisturizers. I wanted something that could finish my evening routine with enough hydration without leaving my skin feeling greasy, sticky, or covered by a heavy layer. That is what led me to this cream on Amazon.

## How I use it

I normally use this as the final step of my evening routine:

**Toner → essence or serum → Torriden DIVE-IN Soothing Cream**

A dime-sized amount is usually enough for my face. I do not apply an especially thick layer.

The texture feels lightweight, fresh, and full of moisture. It has a smooth, slightly slippery feel while I spread it, but it absorbs well and does not remain sticky.

This is one of the biggest reasons I enjoy using it. Many products labeled as soothing creams come in jars and can feel fairly rich. This one comes in a tube, which feels cleaner, more hygienic, and easier to use.

## What it does for my dry, somewhat sensitive skin

The most noticeable benefit is straightforward hydration without irritation.

It helps with the dehydrated feeling in my skin and gives me enough moisture to comfortably finish my nighttime routine. I have not experienced stinging, redness, clogged pores, or breakouts while using it.

It feels gentle and uncomplicated. There is no dramatic overnight transformation, but it consistently gives my skin the comfortable layer of moisture I want at the end of my routine.

## Is the hydration long-lasting?

The hydration lasts a moderate amount of time, but I would not describe it as exceptionally long-lasting.

I normally apply toner, essence or serum, and then this cream, so I have not tested it completely on its own. Within that layered routine, it performs well as the final moisturizer.

Because it has very little oily or occlusive feeling, people with severely dry skin may find it insufficient, especially during winter. I have used it comfortably in warmer weather, but I have not yet tested it through winter. Based on its lightweight finish, I may need something richer over it when the weather becomes very dry.

## Does it feel greasy or heavy?

No. This is one of its strongest points.

The cream is lightweight, non-sticky, and low in oiliness. It absorbs without leaving the uncomfortable, heavy finish I sometimes get from richer soothing creams.

I normally use it before bed, so I cannot fairly judge how it behaves underneath makeup. I have not experienced pilling in my nighttime routine, but I will not claim anything about makeup wear until I have actually tested it that way.

## What I like most

- It provides comfortable hydration without heaviness.
- It helps relieve the dehydrated feeling in my dry skin.
- It absorbs well and does not remain sticky.
- It leaves very little greasy or oily residue.
- It has caused no stinging, redness, clogging, or breakouts for me.
- The tube packaging feels cleaner and more convenient than a jar.
- It works especially well after a hydrating toner and serum.

## What could be better

The main drawback is that the moisturizing effect is not extremely long-lasting. Very dry skin may need a richer cream or an additional layer, particularly in cold or dry weather.

The texture also feels slightly slippery before it absorbs. That sensation disappears as it settles, but it is noticeable during application.

## Who may like it?

I think this cream could suit many skin types, particularly combination skin or oily skin looking for a soothing moisturizer that does not feel heavy.

It also works for my dry, somewhat sensitive skin when used as the final step of a layered routine. People with severely dry skin may find it too light on its own. Very oily skin may still prefer to use a smaller amount, depending on how much hydration the rest of the routine provides.

## Would I buy it again?

Most likely, yes.

It is not the cheapest moisturizer, but I am satisfied with the performance for the price. Unless I find something noticeably better, I would purchase it again. If I see it on sale, I may even buy another pack before finishing the tubes I already have.

## Final verdict

The Torriden DIVE-IN Soothing Cream is a light, fresh moisturizer that replenishes hydration without feeling sticky, greasy, or heavy.

For me, it works best as the uncomplicated final step after toner and serum. It is gentle on my dry, somewhat sensitive skin, has caused no irritation or breakouts, and gives me the comfortable moisture I want at night.

**In one sentence:** A gentle, lightweight soothing cream that adds fresh hydration without making my skin feel heavy or suffocated.
