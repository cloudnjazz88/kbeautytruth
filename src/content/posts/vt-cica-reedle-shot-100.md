I bought VT COSMETICS CICA Reedle Shot 100 myself after seeing countless recommendations advising beginners to start with the 100 strength rather than jumping straight into higher concentrations. I have dry, somewhat sensitive skin and tend to be cautious before trying anything active or stimulating.

## How the First Use Actually Felt (The Prickle Factor)

When I first applied Reedle Shot 100, there was an immediate, distinct prickling or tingling sensation. 

VT describes Reedle Shot as using microscopic mineral-derived spicules. I am repeating the brand's mechanism language here, not independently proving that it creates channels or drives ingredients deeper.

What I can say from my own use is simpler. After I applied it as the first step on bare skin, the tingling often felt stronger once I layered toner, essence, and cream over it. Later products sometimes felt more intense on my skin. That is a sensory impression, not proof of deeper penetration or better efficacy.

It feels unusual at first, but for me, it was a tolerable, prickly sensation rather than a burning allergic reaction.

## My Real Application Routine & Schedule

* **Application step:** Immediately after washing my face on bare, dry skin.
* **Amount:** Exactly 3 pumps in the evening.
* **Frequency:** Roughly once every 3 days (not every night).
* **Bottle lifespan:** It took me about 3 to 4 months to completely empty the first bottle using this schedule.
* **Layering sequence:** Reedle Shot 100 &rarr; Hydrating Toner &rarr; Essence/Serum &rarr; Nourishing Cream / Barrier Moisturizer.

### When I Skipped It
Because my skin is sensitive, consistency didn't mean forcing it every 3rd day without exception. On days when my skin felt irritated, sensitized, or compromised, I intentionally skipped Reedle Shot and focused purely on basic gentle barrier care until my skin calmed down.

## What Changed on My Skin

Reedle Shot 100 is not a magical standalone serum that delivers hydration or anti-aging on its own. For me, its main value was as a first-step booster that made the rest of my routine feel more active on my skin.

1. **Refined Skin Texture:** After regular use every 3 days, my skin texture felt noticeably smoother and better refined.
2. **Stronger Layering Sensation:** The biggest difference for me was how toner, serum, and cream felt after Reedle Shot. Products that had felt like they sat on the surface seemed to sink in more readily, and the finish felt more intense. I am describing how it felt, not claiming independently verified deeper delivery.
3. **Routine Multiplier Feeling:** Instead of buying another expensive serum, using Reedle Shot made the 3 to 4 products I already owned feel more worthwhile in my routine. That is my cost-per-benefit impression, not a measured efficacy upgrade.

## The Downsides & Cautions for Sensitive Skin

Even though I love this product and repurchased it, there are a few realities you need to know before buying:

* **Tingling and temporary redness:** If your skin barrier is thin or highly reactive, you might experience redness and a sharper sting. If you are very nervous, starting with Reedle Shot 50 is a safer entry point.
* **The sensation is common for me:** A mild prickle is part of how this product feels on my skin. Feeling nothing would not prove it failed, and tingling alone does not prove deeper delivery.
* **Gentle pressure only:** Do not aggressively rub it in. Gently spread it across the skin and press softly so you don't unnecessarily irritate your barrier.

## Why I Repurchased It

The reason I bought a second bottle came down to cost-per-benefit:

It suited my dry, somewhat sensitive skin without triggering breakouts. After finishing the first bottle, the tingling-plus-layering experience and smoother-feeling texture were enough for me to buy it again. I am not claiming that it independently proved deeper absorption or made other formulas medically more effective. It made my routine feel more intense and worthwhile, and that was enough for a repurchase.

## Who It May Suit
* Those who want a first-step booster and can tolerate a mild prickle.
* Combination or normal skin types looking to smooth out rough surface texture.
* Anyone curious about VT's Reedle Shot approach who prefers to start at the 100 strength rather than jump higher.

## Who Should Be Cautious
* Highly compromised, damaged, or peeling skin barriers (repair your barrier with soothing creams first).
* Anyone with active, inflamed cystic breakouts or open lesions.
* Those who cannot tolerate physical prickling sensations during their skincare routine.
