I applied COSRX Advanced Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence after toner in my evening routine and went to bed. By the next morning, the areas of my face where I had applied it were covered in a red rash and small bumps. My face was itchy, too.

I stopped using the essence and took allergy medicine right away. By the following day, my skin was back to normal. Testing confirmed that I am allergic to snail mucin.

That is why this product belongs in my What Didn’t Work notes. This was a short experience with a clear reason to stop, rather than a long-term review of its moisturizing benefits.

## How I used it

I used the essence at night, after toner. I left it on and went to sleep.

The reaction was noticeable when I woke up the next morning. It affected my whole face where I had applied the product, rather than one isolated spot.

## What the reaction looked and felt like

There was a red rash spread across the treated areas, along with small raised bumps. The itching was an important part of the experience. This was not just a few spots that I happened to notice while trying a new product.

At the time, I thought I was having an allergic reaction and took allergy medicine. Testing confirmed the snail mucin allergy, so this is not simply a guess based on finding similar stories online.

I am describing my own symptoms and test result. They do not mean that every bump someone develops after using this essence has the same cause.

## I had the same reaction to a snail mucin mask

This was not limited to the COSRX essence. I have also used a mask containing snail mucin and experienced the same reaction.

Together with the confirmed allergy, that experience matters to how I choose products. Snail mucin is something I need to look for on an ingredient list, whether the product is an essence or a mask. Changing the format does not remove the ingredient I am allergic to.

## What happened after I stopped

I stopped applying the essence. I also took allergy medicine, and my skin was normal again by the next day.

Both details belong in the timeline. Because I took medicine as well as stopping the product, I cannot separate how much each action contributed to the improvement.

I do not have a longer list of benefits and drawbacks to weigh against that reaction. For me, the rash and itching were enough to end the trial.

## What the allergy result means for this review

The confirmed allergy explains why I am writing this as a personal stop-using note. I am not rating the essence as a bad formula for everyone, and I am not comparing its results with another snail product.

There is also published medical reporting of allergic contact dermatitis after use of a COSRX product containing snail secretion filtrate. That report provides context that such reactions have been observed; it does not establish how common they are or diagnose anyone reading this page. [1]

The American Academy of Dermatology explains that contact dermatitis can involve irritation or an allergic reaction, and that testing may help identify an allergic trigger. A rash alone is not enough to identify the responsible ingredient. [2, 3]

## My verdict

COSRX Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence did not work for my skin. I developed an itchy red rash and small bumps after using it overnight, stopped using it, and had a snail mucin allergy confirmed through testing. A mask containing snail mucin gave me the same reaction, too.

This sits alongside my [Medicube Collagen Jelly Cream stop-using note](/breakouts/why-i-stopped-using-medicube-collagen-jelly-cream/) as another product experience that ended with discontinuation. The circumstances are different, and I am not treating them as evidence of a shared ingredient problem.

My useful takeaway is the record itself: what I applied, when the reaction appeared, what it looked and felt like, and what happened next. That is more honest than trying to turn a brief allergic reaction into a full review of a product’s benefits.
