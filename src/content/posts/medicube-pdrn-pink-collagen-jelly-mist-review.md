I bought the medicube PDRN Pink Collagen Glow Jelly Mist Serum because I wanted something I could reach for whenever my skin started to feel dry.

After about one month of using it at least once a day, it has become more than a quick refreshing spray for me. It gives my skin an immediate dewy finish, but the hydration also lasts longer than I expect from a face mist.

I have used plenty of mists that feel good for a few minutes and then seem to disappear. Sometimes my skin even feels tighter after the water evaporates. This one feels different. It leaves my skin comfortable long after the visible moisture is gone.

I also think my overall skin tone has looked brighter with consistent use. I cannot say that PDRN or collagen alone caused that change, especially because this is one part of a larger routine, but the combination of hydration, softness, and glow has been noticeable to me.

## Product at a glance

The medicube PDRN Pink Collagen Glow Jelly Mist Serum is a 100 ml dual-texture spray containing an oil layer and a jelly essence.

Medicube describes the formula as having a 1:9 oil-to-jelly ratio. The ingredient list begins with Collagen Water at 80.9% and also includes glycerin, niacinamide, meadowfoam seed oil, Sodium DNA, several forms of hyaluronic acid, squalane, glutathione, ceramide NP, and other supporting ingredients.

The bottle label lists Sodium DNA, the ingredient Medicube identifies as PDRN. The formula also contains 10.8 ppm collagen according to the official ingredient list.

Those details explain what is in the bottle. They do not establish that topical PDRN or collagen is responsible for every result I noticed, so I treat my experience as a review of the complete formula.

## How I use it

I use this mist at least once a day, but not at one fixed time.

Sometimes I spray it at the end of my skincare routine. I also keep it nearby and use it right before bed or during the day when my skin begins to feel dry.

Two sprays from a distance are usually enough for me. I do not hold it close to my face unless I specifically want a wetter, more concentrated application.

Medicube instructs users to shake the bottle before each use so the oil and jelly layers mix evenly. I mixed it thoroughly when I first opened it, and now I give it a light shake before spraying. I have not noticed the layers clearly separating again between uses, but I still shake it lightly as directed.

## Mist or serum depends on the distance

This is one of the more useful features of the product.

When I spray it from farther away, it spreads across my face like a mist. When I hold it closer, more product lands in one area and it feels closer to applying a light serum.

The spray is not the finest cloud-like mist I have tried. The droplets feel slightly fuller and more moisturizing than a very watery face mist. They are still dispersed well enough that I can use the product without rubbing my face afterward when I spray from a reasonable distance.

I prefer the distant application because it is quick and evenly covers my skin. The closer method makes more sense when I want concentrated hydration and plan to press the product into my skin.

## Texture, scent, and finish

The formula has more substance than plain water.

It feels slightly tacky at first, which surprised me, but the tackiness disappears completely once the formula absorbs. My skin is left soft and hydrated without a sticky film or greasy residue.

The scent is subtle, fresh, and pleasant to me. It does not smell strong or linger in a way that bothers me.

Medicube says the product is free from artificial fragrance and colorants. However, the ingredient list includes lemongrass leaf oil and anise fruit or seed oil, so I would not describe it simply as fragrance-free. Those ingredients may also contribute to the light scent.

## The hydration lasts longer than a typical mist

The biggest difference I noticed is the duration of the hydration.

Many face mists give me a brief splash of moisture but do not do much once they dry. Some even seem to leave my skin feeling tighter as the water evaporates.

This formula feels as though it leaves a very thin protective veil over my skin. That is a description of the sensation, not a claim that it repairs or replaces my skin barrier.

My skin feels hydrated immediately after spraying, and the comfortable feeling lasts instead of disappearing as soon as the visible moisture dries.

I still use moisturizer as part of my regular routine. I would not treat this mist as a complete replacement for a cream, especially when my skin feels particularly dry. Its strength is adding convenient hydration at the end of my routine or later in the day.

## Immediate glow and my longer-term results

The glow appears almost immediately.

Right after spraying, my skin catches more light and looks dewy. Some of that is naturally the surface finish of a moisturizing mist. I do not treat an instant reflective finish as proof of a deeper skin change.

After using the product consistently for about a month, I also think my overall complexion looks brighter. My skin feels smoother and has a softer, bouncier quality.

The changes I noticed were:

* Longer-lasting hydration
* An immediate dewy glow
* Softer skin texture
* A slightly plumper and more elastic feel
* Brighter-looking skin with continued use

I cannot separate the effects of every ingredient or this one product from the rest of my routine. Still, I can confidently say that my skin looks and feels better after I use the complete formula.

## Using it over makeup

I have also used this mist over makeup.

I spray it from a distance only two or three times. Applied that way, it has not made my makeup lift, separate, or require correction.

It adds moisture and glow without soaking one area of my face. I would avoid spraying it too closely over makeup because the larger droplets could leave the surface wetter than intended.

I also would not say that finely broken collagen is what makes it work over makeup. What I can observe is simpler: the mist spreads evenly enough from a distance, settles quickly, and has not disturbed my makeup when I use a small amount.

## Irritation and sensitive skin

I have not experienced stinging, redness, breakouts, or other irritation from this product.

Medicube describes the formula as dermatologist-tested, low irritation, and free from artificial fragrance and colorants. That does not guarantee that every sensitive skin type will tolerate it.

The ingredient list contains botanical oils, including lemongrass and anise oils. Someone who knows those types of ingredients bother their skin may still want to patch test before spraying the product across the entire face.

For my slightly dry, slightly sensitive skin, it has been comfortable so far.

## Where it fits next to the PDRN Pink Peptide Serum

I also use Medicube’s PDRN Pink Peptide Serum, but the two products serve different purposes in my routine.

The Pink Peptide Serum feels thicker and more nourishing, so I use it as a planned skincare step after toner. The Jelly Mist is lighter and more flexible. I can spray it at the end of my routine, before bed, over makeup, or whenever my skin begins to feel dry.

I do not see the mist as a replacement for the serum. The serum provides a more concentrated skincare step, while the mist is easier to use throughout the day.

## What I would change

The spray is slightly fuller than an ultra-fine aerosol-like mist, and the formula feels a little tacky before it absorbs.

Neither issue bothers me. The fuller droplets are part of what makes it feel more substantial than a watery mist, and the tackiness disappears completely after absorption.

Still, someone looking for a completely weightless, invisible spray may notice the difference.

## Is it worth it?

For me, yes.

I bought it because I wanted convenient hydration, and it performs that role better than the quick-drying face mists I have used before.

I like that I can change the application by adjusting the distance. I can use it as a light finishing mist, a more concentrated serum-like spray, or a quick moisture boost over makeup.

The immediate glow is attractive, but the lasting hydration is what makes me want to repurchase it. The softer texture and brighter-looking complexion I noticed over the month are additional benefits.

I would recommend it most to someone who wants:

* A mist that feels more moisturizing than water
* A dewy finish
* An easy hydration step during the day
* A product that can be used over makeup
* A lighter alternative to applying another layer of serum

I would be more cautious if you dislike any temporary tackiness, prefer completely scent-free formulas, or know that aromatic plant oils irritate your skin.

## The short answer

The medicube PDRN Pink Collagen Glow Jelly Mist Serum gives me an immediate glow, but it does more than make my skin look temporarily wet.

Its slightly richer mist leaves my skin hydrated, soft, and comfortable for longer than most face mists I have tried. After about one month of daily use, I also think my complexion looks brighter and my skin feels smoother and bouncier.

The spray is not ultra-fine, and it feels slightly tacky for a moment before absorbing. Once it settles, however, the tackiness disappears completely.

The lasting hydration is what makes this feel like a mist that actually does something rather than one that simply evaporates.
