I originally bought **mixsoon Galactomyces Toner** because I wanted a lightweight toner that I could use without making my routine feel heavy. I purchased it from Amazon, finished the first bottle, and now use my second bottle every day.

That probably says more about my experience than an elaborate claim could. If it had caused breakouts or felt unpleasant on my sensitive skin, I would not have bought another 300 ml bottle.

## My skin and how I use it

I have dry, somewhat sensitive skin. I use this toner as the first step of my routine, after cleansing and before any essence or serum.

Because the bottle is large and the texture is so light, I do not measure out a careful number of drops. I apply it generously. It feels almost exactly like water, spreads easily, and absorbs quickly instead of sitting on top of my skin.

## The texture and finish

The first thing I notice when I apply it is how fresh it feels. The watery texture disappears into my skin quickly, but it does not leave my face feeling as though I applied nothing.

After it absorbs, my skin feels softer and the surface feels more even and tidy. What keeps me using it is the balance: it feels very light while still giving me a satisfying amount of hydration.

## What I layer after it

Since this is my first step, the products that follow can change from one routine to another. I sometimes use **Torriden DIVE-IN Serum** next and sometimes use **medicube PDRN Pink Peptide Serum**.

I have also used the toner in routines that included **The Ordinary Salicylic Acid 2% Solution** or **The Ordinary Retinol 0.2% in Squalane**. In my own routine, I did not notice any discomfort or resistance when using them in the same routine.

That is only a record of how my skin responded. It is not a promise that every sensitive skin type will react the same way, especially when active products are involved.

## Did it cause any irritation or breakouts?

No. I have not experienced breakouts from this toner, and I have never needed to stop using it to let my skin recover.

My skin is sensitive, so I normally research products carefully before buying them. A product does not need to create a dramatic overnight change for me to consider it successful. Being comfortable enough to use every day, fitting easily with the rest of my routine, and making my skin feel good are meaningful advantages.

## What I like most

- The texture is genuinely watery and lightweight.
- It feels fresh when applied.
- It absorbs quickly.
- My skin feels softer and smoother afterward.
- It gives me good hydration without making the first step feel heavy.
- It has fit comfortably into different versions of my routine.

## Is there anything I do not like?

I do not have a personal failure or irritation to report. This is also not a toner I bought for a dramatic treatment-style effect. I wanted a light, easy toner, and that is the role it fills for me.

## Will I repurchase it?

Yes. I am already using my second bottle, and I will most likely purchase it again when this one is empty.

It suits my skin, feels effortless to use, and gives me the combination of quick absorption, softness, and hydration that I want from an everyday toner. For me, it is the kind of product that becomes valuable because I can keep reaching for it without having to think too much about it.

## Final verdict

**mixsoon Galactomyces Toner is an everyday repurchase for me, not a miracle-product story.** It is watery, fast-absorbing, comfortable on my dry and somewhat sensitive skin, and more hydrating than its very light texture initially suggests.

My experience is based on finishing one bottle and continuing with a second. That longer, uneventful use is exactly why I trust it enough to keep it in my routine.
