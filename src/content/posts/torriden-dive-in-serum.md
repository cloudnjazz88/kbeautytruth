I bought **Torriden DIVE-IN Low Molecular Hyaluronic Acid Serum** because I was looking for something that would absorb easily and help with the dry, tight feeling I get beneath the surface of my skin.

I am about halfway through my first bottle. I originally bought a two-pack from Amazon, so I already have another bottle waiting. Based on how it has worked so far, I expect I will buy it again after I finish both.

This is not a dramatic treatment serum for me. It is the basic hydration step that makes my dry skin feel more comfortable, especially on days when I wear makeup.

## How I use it in my evening routine

I usually apply the serum at night after showering. My order is:

1. **mixsoon Galactomyces Toner**
2. **Torriden DIVE-IN Serum**
3. **Torriden DIVE-IN Hyaluronic Acid Soothing Cream**

I let the toner absorb before moving on to the serum. Then I apply about three or four drops and gently pat it in. It absorbs quickly when I tap it into my skin.

## What the texture actually feels like

The serum is mostly watery, with just a little viscosity. It has a slightly slippery feel as it spreads, but it is not oily or heavy.

That balance is one of the reasons I like it. It gives me the feeling of adding a real hydration layer without making the rest of my routine feel thick.

Once it absorbs, my skin feels hydrated rather than coated. I have not experienced stickiness, pilling, or an oily finish.

## What I notice right after applying it

The most immediate difference is comfort. My skin feels filled with moisture, the tight feeling eases, and the surface looks and feels smoother.

The hand test shows a fresh sheen right after application, but the serum does not leave behind the kind of greasy film I associate with an oil-based product.

## The difference I notice when wearing makeup

Dryness under makeup is one of the clearest ways I can tell whether a hydration product is helping me. Before adding this serum, my skin could start to feel tight after I had been wearing makeup for a while.

Since using it, I do not get that same level of tightness. My skin stays more comfortable beneath makeup instead of feeling progressively drier as the day goes on.

I am describing how my skin feels, not claiming that the serum changes makeup wear for everyone. For my dry skin, though, this has been one of its most useful effects.

## Why I do not stop at the serum step

This serum adds water-like hydration, but it is not enough to finish my routine by itself. If I stop after the serum, my skin can start to feel dry again a few hours later.

Following it with a cream makes a noticeable difference for me. My favorite combination has been the mixsoon toner, this serum, and Torriden DIVE-IN Hyaluronic Acid Soothing Cream on top.

That distinction matters: I like the serum very much, but I do not treat it as a replacement for moisturizer. On my dry skin, it works best as the hydrating middle layer.

## Irritation, breakouts, and layering

I have dry, somewhat sensitive skin, and I have not experienced irritation or breakouts from this serum.

It has also layered well with the other products in my routine. I have not noticed pilling or stickiness. I do let each layer absorb, but the serum itself sinks in quickly, so the routine does not require a long wait.

## What I like most

- It feels light and is not oily.
- Three or four drops are enough for my face.
- It absorbs quickly when patted in.
- It does not feel sticky after absorption.
- It helps ease the tight, dehydrated feeling in my dry skin.
- My skin feels smoother and more comfortable after applying it.
- It has not caused irritation, breakouts, or pilling for me.
- It works especially well beneath a cream.

## What could be better?

I do not have a specific complaint about the serum itself. The main limitation is simply that it is not my final moisturizing step.

Someone with very dry skin may find it too light on its own, as I do. That does not make it ineffective for me; it just means I need to layer a cream over it.

## Is it worth the price?

I think the regular price is reasonable, and it can be a particularly good value when it is on sale. Buying the two-pack made sense for me because this is an uncomplicated serum I can use regularly rather than something I reach for only occasionally.

Prices and promotions change, so I would compare the current options rather than relying on the amount I paid.

## Will I buy it again?

Most likely, yes. I still have the second bottle from my two-pack to use, but once both are empty, I expect to purchase it again.

It is affordable, comfortable, and does exactly what I wanted from a basic hydration serum. I do not have a reason to remove it from my routine.

## Who I think it may suit

Because the texture is light, non-oily, and easy to layer, I think it could be appealing to many skin types looking for an uncomplicated hydration step.

My direct experience is with dry, somewhat sensitive skin. If your skin is very dry, the serum alone may not feel sufficient. I would plan to use a moisturizer afterward rather than expecting it to replace one.

## Final verdict

**Torriden DIVE-IN Serum is the kind of basic product I end up using consistently.** It is not about a flashy overnight transformation. It gives my dry skin a light, fast-absorbing layer of hydration, helps reduce the tight feeling I used to notice under makeup, and sits comfortably between toner and cream.

For me, its best quality is that it does its job without adding weight, oiliness, irritation, or fuss.
