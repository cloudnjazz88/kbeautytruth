I wanted to like medicube Collagen Jelly Cream. It spread nicely, and its thick, cushiony jelly texture felt as though it would hold moisture in well. But after using it at night, I sometimes woke up to a very different result.

Small, red, rash-like bumps appeared on my cheeks, and my skin felt hot. The first time, I didn’t know what had caused it. When it happened again, I still wasn’t certain. By the third similar reaction, after using the cream again, I couldn’t ignore the pattern.

## What happened when I stopped

I stopped using the jelly cream, and I didn’t notice new episodes of that reaction afterward. When my skin was unsettled, I used AESTURA ATOBARRIER365 Cream. It took about two days for my skin to look and feel like itself again.

That doesn’t mean AESTURA treated the rash, or that I know exactly what caused it. I can only say what I observed: the reaction followed my use of the jelly cream more than once, and it stopped recurring after I took the product out of my routine.

## What I liked, and why I still stopped

The application itself was pleasant. I liked how easily the cream spread, and the substantial texture made me think it might be a good way to keep my dry skin moisturized. But a promising texture isn’t worth waking up with red, heated cheeks.

I don’t know whether a particular ingredient, the formula as a whole, or something else was responsible. I’m not saying this cream will cause the same reaction for everyone. **It just didn’t work for my skin**, and after seeing the same pattern repeatedly, I chose not to use it again.

**Kay’s take:** A cream can feel lovely when you put it on and still be wrong for your skin the next morning. For me, that was reason enough to stop.
