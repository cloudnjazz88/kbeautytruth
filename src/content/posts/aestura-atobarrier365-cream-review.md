I’ve used AESTURA ATOBARRIER365 Cream for about two months. I use it regularly, usually alternating it with my Torriden DIVE-IN Soothing Cream because I like both and don’t need to layer two creams at once. But when my dry, somewhat sensitive skin feels unsettled or I notice a new bump, AESTURA is the one I reach for.

That’s why it has become one of my personal K-beauty favorites.

## How it feels on my skin

I use roughly a pinky-nail-sized amount and spread it in a thin layer over my face. It has a little richness to it, but it doesn’t feel thick or heavy to me. I can feel tiny capsules as I apply it; they soften as I smooth the cream over my skin. Afterward, my face feels moisturized, almost as though it has a light protective layer over it.

## Why I trust it on sensitive-feeling days

More than once, I’ve noticed a bump before work, applied this cream, and found that my skin looked much calmer when I got home. After using it again that night, the bump was gone by the next morning. I can’t know for certain that the cream alone caused the change, but having a cream I feel comfortable reaching for on those days means a lot to me.

My mom also tried it when she had a rash. Her skin settled over the next few days while she was using it. I don’t know what caused her rash or whether the cream was responsible for the improvement, but that experience added to my confidence in how comfortable this formula feels for us.

## AESTURA or Torriden?

I enjoy using both, and I alternate between them rather than applying both creams together. I could happily use AESTURA every day; I simply have another cream I like, too. When my skin feels especially sensitive or a bump appears, I’m more likely to choose AESTURA.

Based on my experience, it’s a cream I’d especially consider if you have dry, sensitive-feeling skin, though no product will suit everyone.

My main hesitation is the price in the U.S. A small amount goes a long way for me, which makes it easier to justify, but it still costs more than I’d like. I’ve also found that makeup can pill if I apply it before the cream has fully settled, so I give it time to absorb first.

**Kay’s take:** I use this cream regularly, but on days when my skin feels unsettled, it’s the one I trust most.
