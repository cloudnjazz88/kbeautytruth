## Why Torriden stays in my routine

Torriden is easy for me to reach for when I want hydration without a heavy or sticky finish. I often use it as the last step of my evening routine, after lighter hydrating products. It feels fresh once absorbed, though there can be a briefly slippery feeling while I’m applying it.

On my dry skin, its moisturizing feel doesn't last as long as AESTURA's. I also haven't tried using it as my only skincare step, so I can't say how it would perform alone.

## When I choose AESTURA instead

I use AESTURA regularly too, not just when something goes wrong. A small, thin layer gives my skin a little more richness without feeling thick or suffocating. Unlike Torriden, it has a little oiliness that leaves what feels like a thin layer over my skin. For me, that makes the moisturized feeling last noticeably longer.

When my skin feels sensitive or I notice a bump, it’s also the cream I feel most confident reaching for. I've seen my skin look calmer after using it on those days. That's my experience, not a promise that a cream will treat breakouts or work the same way for everyone.

## Kay's take

If I want a light, fresh moisturizer, I choose Torriden. If my skin feels unsettled or I want moisture that lasts longer, I choose AESTURA. I keep both because I genuinely enjoy using both, and alternating them works better for me than putting two creams on at once.

If you’re deciding between them, I'd start with the finish you actually like wearing. The best cream isn't the one that wins a comparison chart. It's the one you'll feel comfortable using.
