## Morning

After cleanser and toner, I use **COSRX Advanced Pure Vitamin C 23% Serum**. Then I finish with moisturizer and sunscreen. That is my usual morning path: keep it steady, and do not pile on extra treatments before the day starts.

## Evening

At night I cleanse and tone first. Then I usually choose about two serums or treatments from what I already keep on my shelf:

- medicube PDRN Pink Peptide Serum with Salmon DNA ([my PDRN note](/ingredients/does-pdrn-skincare-work/))
- The Ordinary Retinol 0.2% in Squalane ([how I start retinol](/ingredients/retinol-for-beginners/))
- The Ordinary Niacinamide 10% + Zinc 1%
- [Torriden DIVE-IN Serum](/reviews/torriden-dive-in-serum/)

I do not use the same two every night. I pick by how my skin feels that day. Retinol is not daily for me—I use it about once every three days.

Eye cream is optional. I use it sometimes, not as a required step.

About every other day, I also use the medicube Booster Pro X2 with Holika Holika Aloe 99% Soothing Gel. On those nights I still usually keep my two serum choices. The booster and aloe gel go on before moisturizer.

## Moisturizer

For the last cream, I rotate [Torriden DIVE-IN Soothing Cream](/reviews/torriden-dive-in-soothing-cream/) and [AESTURA ATOBARRIER365 Cream](/reviews/aestura-atobarrier365-cream-review/). Torriden is the one I reach for when I want a lighter finish. AESTURA is the one I reach for when my skin feels sensitive or I want longer-lasting moisture.

## Kay’s take

I am not trying to win at step count. Some days my routine is shorter. Some evenings I add the optional pieces. What matters for my dry, somewhat sensitive skin is choosing what fits that day, then stopping when my face feels comfortable—not forcing a longer or shorter routine because a trend says I should.
